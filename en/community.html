Keeping the feed worth opening

Community Guidelines

Blurt is one daily question answered by everyone. That only works if the feed stays safe, honest, and worth opening. These guidelines explain what is not allowed. They are part of our Terms of Service.

Keep it legal

  • No content that violates the law or promotes illegal activity, including drugs, weapons, or gambling.
  • No sexual or pornographic content, and no nudity in answers, photos, or videos.
  • No dangerous acts, self-harm promotion, or graphic violence.

Be respectful

  • No harassment, hate speech, bullying, or threats targeting anyone, including in comments and anonymous replies.
  • No impersonation of other people, brands, or public figures.
  • No sharing of another person's private information (photos, contact details, location) without consent.

Own what you post

  • Only post content you created or have the rights to.
  • Don't post other people's likeness or personal data without their permission.
  • No spam, scams, pyramid schemes, or crypto and financial promotions.

Keep it human

  • No bots, automation, or scraping.
  • Don't create multiple accounts to evade limits or moderation.

How enforcement works

Every answer, comment, and profile has a Report action. Reports are reviewed against these guidelines. Depending on the violation we may remove the content, mark an account as deleted, or restrict access to Blurt. Serious violations may result in immediate removal without warning.

Blocking

You can Block anyone from the "···" menu on any answer or from the "···" menu on their profile. Blocked users' answers stop appearing in your feed, and you can unblock them at any time in Settings → Blocked Users.

Contact

To flag something urgent, or if you believe a moderation decision was wrong, email blurt.today@gmail.com.
